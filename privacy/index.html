Who we are

UXONIC Digital Solutions (“UXONIC”, “we”, “us”) is a digital agency and software company based in Trichy and Karur, Tamil Nadu, India. You can reach us at info@uxonicdigital.com or +91 98430 21717.

Two kinds of data

Your account data. When you sign up for UXONIC Books, contact us or apply for a job, we decide how that data is used. For this data we are the Data Fiduciary.

Your business records. The leads, customers, invoices, products, stock, employees, attendance and other records your business enters in UXONIC Books belong to your business. Your business decides what goes in and who can see it; we store and process it only to run the service for you. For this data your business is the Data Fiduciary and we act as its Data Processor. If you are a customer or employee of a business that uses UXONIC Books, please contact that business first about your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile name and photo. We never see your Google password.
  • Workspace details: business name, address, phone, GSTIN, logo and settings you add.
  • Business records you or your team enter (see above).
  • Location at check-in: only in workspaces that use UXONIC HR, and only when an employee taps “Check in” with location allowed in the browser. We save that single position with the check-in. We do not track location in the background.
  • Payments: the plan, amount, date, method and reference of subscription payments. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner (Razorpay) and are never stored by us.
  • Support messages you send us from the app, by email or WhatsApp.
  • Website forms: details you submit through the contact form or a job or internship application, including any resume you upload.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and security logs (for example sign-in times) that keep accounts safe.

How we use it

  • To provide UXONIC Books: sign-in, workspaces, the features you use, and emails the service sends (invitations, reminders, invoices your business sends to its customers).
  • To bill your subscription, send trial and renewal reminders, and answer support requests.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To reply to enquiries and job applications sent through our website.
  • To improve our products, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use your business records for advertising, and we do not show ads in UXONIC Books.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run UXONIC Books, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage. Data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in the Tokyo, Japan region.
  • Vercel — hosting of our website and app.
  • Google — “Sign in with Google”, if you choose it.
  • Resend — delivery of the emails the service sends.
  • Razorpay — online subscription payments.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We use them only where they offer protections at least as strong as this policy. We may also disclose data when Indian law requires it.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each workspace is kept separate at the database level, so one business can never read another’s records.
  • Inside a workspace, roles (admin, manager, employee) decide who can see and change what.
  • Access by UXONIC staff is limited to what is needed to run the service and support you.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • We keep account and workspace data while your account is active.
  • If you ask us to delete a workspace, we delete its records within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed in the following backup cycles.
  • Our own subscription and payment records are kept for up to 8 years, as Indian tax law requires.
  • We keep website enquiries and job applications for up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it (unless we must keep it by law);
  • nominate someone to act for you.

You can also withdraw consent, for example by deleting your account.

Write to info@uxonicdigital.com from the email address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what is needed for the service to work: storage that keeps you signed in and remembers preferences such as the theme or the last workspace you opened. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Children

UXONIC Books is meant for businesses and is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Grievance Officer

For any concern about your personal data or this policy, contact our Grievance Officer at info@uxonicdigital.com (subject: “Privacy”). We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell account holders by email or in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
