Your Role

WHAT YOU'LL DO

As a Sales & Business Development Intern at UXONIC, you'll be at the front line — identifying opportunities, building relationships, and supporting the growth of our client base.

  • Identify and reach out to potential clients
  • Generate qualified business leads
  • Contact prospects through calls, WhatsApp and email
  • Understand client requirements
  • Present UXONIC Digital Solutions services
  • Follow up with prospects
  • Schedule client meetings
  • Support the client conversion process
  • Maintain lead and follow-up records
  • Learn real-world sales and business development strategies
