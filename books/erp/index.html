What UXONIC ERP will do

Planned scope — built for traders, contractors and small manufacturers.

Products & services

One catalogue with categories and units, shared with your invoice line items.

Stock & warehouses

Stock levels per warehouse or branch, with every movement recorded.

  • Stock in, out and transfer
  • Adjustments with a reason
  • Low-stock alerts

Suppliers & purchases

Purchase orders to suppliers and goods-received notes when stock arrives.

Sales orders

Orders that reserve stock and flow straight into invoices.

Barcode labels

Print labels for products and scan them in and out.

Branch reports

Stock value, movement and sales by branch.

How we plan to roll it out

Released in stages so each part is solid before the next one ships. Early-access members help shape the order.

Stage 1

Catalogue & stock

  • Products, categories, units
  • Warehouses and stock levels
  • Stock movements and adjustments
Stage 2

Buying

  • Suppliers
  • Purchase orders
  • Goods received and low-stock alerts
Stage 3

Selling & branches

  • Sales orders linked to invoices
  • Barcode labels
  • Branch-wise reports
