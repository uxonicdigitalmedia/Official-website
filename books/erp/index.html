Stock that keeps itself up to date

Made for shops, traders, distributors and contractors who are tired of counting on paper.

Products & services

One list with SKU, barcode, HSN, GST rate, unit, selling price and cost.

  • Categories and units (pcs, kg, m, box…)
  • Goods with stock, services without
  • Shared with your invoice lines

Stock per godown

See what is in hand in each godown or branch — and the total.

  • Stock in, stock out, physical count
  • Move stock between godowns
  • Opening stock in minutes

Linked to invoices

Issue a GST invoice and the items leave stock automatically. Void it and they come back.

  • Pick products on the invoice
  • Choose which godown it ships from
  • No double entry

Suppliers & purchase orders

Raise a PO, mark it ordered, then receive goods — all at once or in parts.

  • Automatic PO numbers per year
  • Partly received tracking
  • Received stock costed at the PO rate

Low-stock alerts

Set a reorder level per product and see what to buy before you run out.

  • Low and out-of-stock lists
  • On your dashboard every morning
  • Stock value at cost

Barcode labels

Print Code 128 labels with name and price; scan to find a product instantly.

  • A4 sheets, 3 labels per row
  • Works with any USB barcode scanner
  • Your own codes or SKUs
Stock history

Every piece, accounted for

Stock only changes through a recorded entry — a sale, a purchase, a count or a transfer — with who did it and why. Entries cannot be edited later, so the numbers always add up.

  • Linked back to the invoice or purchase order
  • Reason on every manual change
  • Filter by godown, product or type
