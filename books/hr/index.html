Everything a small team actually needs

Built for shops, offices, clinics and site teams — not for a 500-person HR department.

Employee profiles

Code, department, designation, shift, joining date, weekly off and emergency contact — in one place.

  • With or without an app login
  • Reports-to for each person
  • Former staff kept, not deleted

Check-in & check-out

Staff tap Check in on their phone. Late marks and half days are worked out for you.

  • Grace minutes per shift
  • Optional location on check-in
  • Night shifts supported

Manager corrections

Forgot to check in? Managers mark or correct any past day — with a note, and a record of who changed it.

  • Present, half day, absent, leave
  • Staff without phones too
  • Every change in the audit log

Leave & balances

Casual, sick and loss-of-pay out of the box. Add your own types and yearly allowance.

  • Half-day leave
  • Balances checked automatically
  • Weekly offs and holidays not counted

One-tap approvals

Managers get a notification and approve or reject in a tap. The employee hears back straight away.

  • Managers can’t approve their own
  • Cancel future leave
  • Leave on behalf of staff

Monthly register

The whole month for everyone on one screen — present, half day, absent, leave, holiday and week off.

  • Late count and hours worked
  • CSV export for payroll
  • Holidays for your state
Check-in

One tap. Done.

Each employee sees their own day: check-in time, hours worked and whether they were late. No biometric machine, no paper register, no WhatsApp “reached office” messages.

  • Works on any phone browser
  • Late after start time + grace minutes
  • Less than half a shift becomes a half day
