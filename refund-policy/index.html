Try it free first

Every UXONIC Books plan starts with a free trial. No card is needed. Use it with your real business before you decide to pay.

No cancellation after payment

  • When you pay for 1 month or 1 year, that paid period cannot be cancelled.
  • We do not refund payments for a period that has been paid, fully or partly, including if you stop using the app in the middle.
  • You can change to a bigger plan any time. The new plan starts from your next payment.

How to stop

We never charge you automatically. To stop, simply do not renew when your paid period ends. Your workspace becomes read-only, and you can still see and download your data.

When we do refund

  • Charged twice or by mistake: write to info@uxonicdigital.com within 30 days with the payment reference. We refund the extra amount to the same payment method (UPI, card or bank) within 7 working days of checking it.
  • If UXONIC stops the service for reasons that are not your fault, we refund the unused part of your paid period.

Website and app development projects

For custom websites, apps and software we build for you, the advance and stage payments in your signed quotation apply. Work that has been started is not refundable.
